Amendment 2026-11-09 (before the re-launch, user-approved): the first attempt (seed 52001, check base 53000, started 2026-11-08 23:50) was stopped after about 80 minutes and 6 generations because a rules bug was found in proyecto/symmetric_engine.py: answering truco with retruco (or retruco with vale cuatro) did not accept the lower bet, so envido stayed legal after a retruco and a later fold scored 1 point short (taken in about 0.26% of decisions). A full review then found that training and validation deals could overlap, that the promotion interval ignored the correlation between panel members sharing deals, and that an interrupted champion export could diverge from the checkpoint. All were fixed before the re-launch (see the engine-fix ADR). The thresholds, pair counts and all other settings above are unchanged. The aborted attempt is not a retry for selection purposes: its outputs are archived in TP-IA-Truco-training-archive/phase2-run1-aborted-engine-bug and are not used. Two rule simplifications are confirmed by the user and documented: bets only on the caller's own card turn or as an answer to a pending bet; envido va primero allowed as an answer to the first truco until the first trick completes. Two inference fast paths (bit-identical) were added for speed.
