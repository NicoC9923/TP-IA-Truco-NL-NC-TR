PHASE 2 MEASURING RUN: PRE-REGISTERED RULE
Written before either segment. No retries, reselection, or post-hoc threshold changes.

Segments A and B run in that order for a 480-second trainer time cap each (the cap is checked at round boundaries and can be exceeded by one round). Both use seed 42001 for A and 42002 for B, mixed targets, default league, panel v0, 12 workers, T=0.1, 240 matches per round and generation, 100 panel pairs, 200 direct pairs, and 1000 maximum generations. A disables the auxiliary head; B enables it with 400 validation situations. Generation one establishes the champion, so at least three completed generations are required for two checks; report a failed measurement if fewer occur. Outputs are isolated and no existing output is overwritten. Check schedules use bases 43000 and 45000 respectively, with consecutive seeds per check; these ranges, training seeds 42001-42004, benchmark check seeds 47000 and 48000, replay schedule 49000, and replay action seeds 50000-50017 are fresh relative to the reserved list and default 10000-based check schedule. The 1000-generation cap bounds each check range to 43000-43998 and 45000-45998. Training deal seeds use the trainer's odd-seed scheme; schedule seeds generate even deal seeds.

Each segment records progress.json, generations.jsonl and snapshots. Report whole-segment matches/s and samples/s as progress totals divided by elapsed, also the last-five-round progress rates, and each generation's elapsed_seconds (including a partial final generation); head-off logs stop before the check, so add the benchmark mean check to checked generation durations. Measure check time, validation time and learner milliseconds/update independently with bench_phase2.py once per head, 12 workers, 240 matches per round, otherwise the same panel, target, pairs, minibatch and temperature; the benchmarks use training seeds 42003/42004 and check bases 47000/48000, two generations and its own 175-second watchdog. Validation is the benchmark's mean of cold and warm times, zero when off. These are independent estimates, not direct timings of segment checks. No timing instrumentation changes the trainer.

For each head use complete checked generations: training throughput R = sum(matches) / sum(training seconds), where head-off training seconds are logged elapsed before the check and head-on training seconds are logged elapsed minus benchmark mean check and validation. Let O be benchmark mean check plus validation. For each positive multiple N of 240 with 300 <= N/R + O <= 600 and O/(N/R + O) <= 0.15, choose the one minimizing |N/R + O - 450|, ties to smaller N. Report infeasible if none exists; do not invent a setting. A partial or unchecked generation is excluded from R.

Offline replay uses the final completed B generation snapshot: one fixed mixed-target match each against itself and the four frozen phase 1 league networks for temperature, and one match each against four styled copies (bluffer, calculating, cautious, aggressive, strength 0.1) and those four phase 1 networks for profile scoring. Schedule seed 49000 and action seeds 50000-50017 are fixed; reset each opponent's profile per match. The same 9 replays serve both analyses, with self excluded from the prior and styles excluded from temperature. For every learner decision compute softmax of legal Q at 0.1, report median maximum probability and the fraction of sampled actions with Q more than 0.3 below best legal Q (descriptive only). Choose 0.2 above 0.98, 0.05 below 0.40, else 0.1 inclusive.

Profile scoring uses the actual phase 2 OpponentProfile observation rules, including revealed-hand-only bluff evidence. For each opponent and rate separately, use its first k opportunities as the profile and predict opportunity k+1, for every k >= 1, with neutral beta prior strength 20, 10 or 5. Score every held-out binary outcome once per strength and choose lowest pooled mean log loss; exact ties favor 20, then 10, then 5. Recency weighting is not compared in this pilot.

Keep the auxiliary head if B whole-segment matches/s is at least half A and the final B validation learned log loss is strictly lower than exact; otherwise turn it off. Use the chosen head's generation size. This rule is descriptive pilot evidence, not a causal head comparison. SHA-256 the last completed B snapshot and generate panel-v1.json with the three existing panel members plus that snapshot, with paths relative to the panel file. Archive the snapshot with that panel before moving outputs. Analysis outputs results.json and summary.txt alongside the panel. No real run has yet occurred.
