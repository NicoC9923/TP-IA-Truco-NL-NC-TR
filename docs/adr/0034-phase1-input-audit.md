# Phase 1 network input audit

Status: accepted and pre-registered before the audit run.

The phase 1 population plays differently across individuals but remains weak against Adaptive Utility; recent diagnostics did not establish that selection noise or genetic operators explain the plateau. Before changing inputs or capacity, evaluate the frozen generation 70 population and the three existing reigning champions on a common observation bank. This is a diagnostic, not training or a promotion decision.

Use at least 2,000 card-play decisions generated from population matches on training-partition deals, using the reserved odd seed namespace `2*(10^20 + match_index)+1`, and a fresh default opponent profile for every encoded observation. Audit the shipped champion, the seed 1 champion, the 53% gate seed 1 champion, and 16 uniformly sampled population members without replacement, using legal-action softmax probabilities. The bank and population samples use fixed seeds. The bank may contain additional non-card decisions for mistake checks; seed namespaces do not guarantee deal-disjointness.

## Pre-registered interpretation

Apply H1 and H2 separately to each network and H3 once to the bank; report all results and sample counts, including networks with insufficient opportunities. Missing or undefined statistics cannot support a hypothesis.

1. **H1, card strength:** support if the ratio of mistake rates for face-value/Truco-order-disagreeing versus agreeing pairs is at least 2 and the lower bound of its decision-bootstrap 95% confidence interval is at least 2, or if the mean controlled-probe preference correlation with face value exceeds that with true Truco strength. Pairwise mistakes compare winning with losing cards conditional on selecting either; equal face values are excluded. The probe sweeps card identities in one slot while keeping other encoded inputs fixed, so it may produce impossible combinations.
2. **H2, per-card information:** among decisions where the best card's next-trick win probability exceeds the next best by at least 0.3, support if the best-card argmax rate is at least 0.10 higher when that card is the first available card than when it is another card, and the lower bound of the normal 95% confidence interval for that difference is positive. Compare card actions conditional on playing a card; tied argmax choices split credit. The model uses exact outcomes against an already played card, otherwise existing inference with a uniformly chosen remaining opponent card and strict wins.
3. **H3, aliasing:** support if at least 5% of bank observations have a strategically different observation within L2 distance 0.01 in encoded space. Compare held-card Truco ranks, legal actions and envido points, and also report exact aliases. Near groups are connected components of differing-fact pairs and can have endpoints farther apart than 0.01.
4. Report clear-mistake probabilities and qualifying opportunity counts without a fixed support threshold. These are limited to the implemented sufficient conditions; an absent opportunity is not evidence of good play.

Report H1 and H2 support for each network, H3 for the bank, and the number of networks meeting each rule. If no rule fires, conclude **no input diagnosis**. If any rule fires, propose only one concrete input change, selected in this fixed priority order: for H1, replace each card's normalized face-value input with normalized Truco rank; for H2, add the next-trick win probability for each of the other two card slots; for H3, append the twelve legal-action indicators. H1 retains 64 inputs; H2 also fits within the 64 inputs by consuming two padding slots; H3 uses the seven existing padding slots and requires 69 inputs. These are proposals, not changes to the encoder: each breaks compatibility with existing genomes and panel, requires from-scratch training and a new ADR, and may require a spec #15 change.

The diagnostics use one frozen population, finite observations and simplified opponent inference. Decisions within a hand and policy outputs are correlated; the stated bootstrap and normal intervals do not account for all such dependence, so results are descriptive rather than causal evidence or independent confirmation. A high-envido decline is a heuristic mistake check, and accepting known losing envido may have no opportunities under the current engine's declaration timing. Neither the reference opponents nor the diagnostic enter training.
